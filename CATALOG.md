# Real public datasets for the hackathon

69 datasets you can use during the event. Each link and licence was checked on 2026-10-04. The same list is in `catalog.csv`, with full notes on format and coverage.

**Before you build a product on any of these, read the licence.** Entries marked NON-COMMERCIAL, SHARE-ALIKE, RESTRICTED or REQUEST ONLY in their notes have conditions that may matter to a startup. Nothing here is classified or export-controlled, and none of it needs a security clearance.
